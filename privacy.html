Leti — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Leti ("we," "us") is a casual social app for sharing your sports and activity life with friends. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Questions: benwodonnell@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase); we do not store your password in plain text.
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, profile photo, the sports you add, and — only if you choose to add one — a "Lately at" spot (the place you've been practicing recently). It is strictly optional, you can remove it anytime in the app, it clears automatically about three weeks after you stop logging at that place, and on a private account the app doesn't show it to anyone who doesn't follow you.
  • Content you create: posts (text, photos, and videos), sport tags, comments, reactions ("shouts"), practices/activities and competitions you add to your schedule, teams you create or join and what you share there, tags of friends you post with, and direct messages you send.
  • Social connections: the accounts you follow, who follows you, and (on private accounts) follow requests.
  • Safety data: reports you file, the accounts you block or mute, and any feedback notes you send us in the app — kept so we can run the service and keep it safe.
  • Basic technical data: standard app/device and log information needed to run the service and keep it secure.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a device push token so we can deliver the reminders and social alerts you opted into. You can turn these off anytime in iOS Settings.
  • How you use Leti: which screens you visit and which buttons you tap (for example, opening the composer or shouting a post), so we can see what's working and improve the app. This is about actions, not content — we record *that* you sent a comment, never a copy of what it said. This data stays on Leti's own servers, is never sold, and is never shared for advertising; detailed records are deleted after 90 days. We also record which posts you've already been shown, so your feed can avoid re-serving you the same things.

We do not use advertising trackers, and there are no ads in Leti.

Location and places

We use your device location only while you're picking a place for a post or a schedule event. When you open the location picker and have given the app location permission, your device coordinates are sent to Leti's own server, which asks OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for nearby place suggestions and the name of where you are (reverse-geocoding). Place search and map preview images are powered by Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit service on your device): your search text and approximate location are sent to Apple to return suggestions and map images, and Apple handles them under Apple's privacy policy. If Apple Maps can't complete a lookup, we fall back to sending the search through Leti's own server to Nominatim as well. On every path, your device coordinates are processed transiently and are never stored by Leti — we save only the place you pick: its name and that venue's public map coordinates, whether it's attached to a post or a schedule event.

Tagged places get a simple "spot" page — but tapping a place shows logs there only from you and people you follow, never from strangers.

How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the app — create your account, show your posts to the friends and people you've chosen, maintain your feed, streaks, schedule, and profile.
  • To keep the community safe (for example, responding to reports or abuse).
  • To communicate with you about the service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising — that includes minors' data.

Direct messages

You can message friends on Leti (messaging is limited to people who follow each other, and you can turn messages off entirely in Settings). Here's how your messages are handled:

  • Messages are stored on Leti's servers and are visible to you and the people in the conversation. They are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • We do not read your messages. A message may be reviewed by us only for safety — for example, when someone in the conversation reports it — or when the law requires it.
  • Read receipts are off by default and only work when both people have turned them on.
  • Your messages are deleted when you delete your account.

How your content is shared

Your posts are visible according to the visibility you choose (public, followers, or only me) and to people you tag or share a post with. If your account is private, your posts, schedule, and activity are visible only to followers you approve — but your profile header (your name, username, profile photo, bio, and sports) can still be seen by anyone, and an opted-in "Lately at" spot shows to your followers. A shared ("practiced together") post appears on a tagged friend's profile only after they accept it. Posts with a tagged place also appear on that place's spot page — visible only to you and your followers as described above.

We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of Leti users or the public.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run Leti, including:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and photo/video storage (hosted on Amazon Web Services, US).
  • Apple — Apple Maps place search and map images (as described above), the Apple Push Notification service that delivers notifications to your device, and TestFlight/the App Store for distribution.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — helps route the push notifications you opt into. Along with Apple's push service, it delivers your device token and the notification text itself, which can include a piece of user content (for example an event title or a friend's name), plus a link to the right screen in the app and your unread badge count. We don't share anything else with them.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place lookups (nearby suggestions, reverse-geocoding, and fallback search), reached only via Leti's own server; coordinates are processed transiently and no account information is sent.

These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service. We use no third-party analytics or tracking services.

Data storage, security, and retention

Your data is stored with our provider (Supabase). Access to your account data and posts is enforced server-side with row-level security. One honest caveat: photos and videos are served from hard-to-guess links, and anyone who has one of those links can view that media file — your visibility settings protect your posts inside the app, not a media link someone copies out of it.

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete a post, it is removed from the app immediately; the underlying photo and video files may remain in storage until your account is deleted — email us at benwodonnell@gmail.com and we'll remove them sooner. When you delete your account, your data — including your photos and videos — is removed, and residual copies in backups are purged within about 30 days. Your push token is deleted when you sign out or delete your account; turning notifications off for Leti in iOS Settings stops any notifications from being delivered to your device. Detailed usage records are deleted after 90 days (see above). If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime, right in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, posts, photos and videos, messages, and personal data. You can also request deletion by emailing benwodonnell@gmail.com.
  • Edit or remove your profile info (including your home spot) and delete individual posts at any time in the app.
  • Make your account private, turn messages off, and control read receipts — all in Settings.
  • No matter where you live, we honor requests to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data — just email us. We will never treat you differently for exercising these rights.
  • If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision and we'll answer within 60 days. Connecticut residents who are unsatisfied with the outcome of an appeal can contact the Connecticut Attorney General.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Users outside the United States

Leti is operated from the United States and your data is processed and stored in the US. If you use Leti from elsewhere, you consent to your data being processed in the US. The same access and deletion rights above apply to everyone, via the same channels.

Children

Leti is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. We ask for your birthday at sign-up to confirm you're 13 or older; the birthday is checked on your device and is not stored. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can contact us at benwodonnell@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, provide notice in the app.
