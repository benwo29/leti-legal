Leti — Support

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Leti is a casual social app for keeping up with your friends' sports lives — you log a practice, a game, or a pickup run, and your friends see it. This page is how you reach us and how to handle the most common things.

Contact us: benwodonnell@gmail.com

We read every message and reply as quickly as we can — usually within a day. Reports of harmful content are reviewed within 24 hours.

Report something harmful

If you see a post, a comment, or an account that shouldn't be on Leti, you can report it from inside the app:

  • A post: tap the ⋯ menu on the post → Report.
  • A comment: press and hold the comment → Report.
  • An account: open their profile → ⋯ → Report.

Reports alert us immediately. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content or remove the person responsible when it breaks our rules. You can also email us at benwodonnell@gmail.com — include a username or a screenshot and we'll find it.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency services first.

Block or mute someone

You don't have to wait for us. Open the person's profile, tap ⋯, and choose:

  • Block — you disappear from each other completely: no posts, no comments, no messages, no notifications, in either direction.
  • Mute — you stop seeing them in your feed, quietly. They aren't told.

You can undo either one anytime in Settings → Blocked & muted.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account, your posts, your photos, videos and audio clips, your messages, and your personal data. It cannot be undone.

If you can't get into your account, email benwodonnell@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Notifications

Leti sends reminders to log around the time you usually post, a nudge after a practice on your schedule, and alerts when friends interact with you. Every category has its own switch in Settings → Notifications inside the app.

If you don't get any notifications at all, check that they're allowed for Leti at the phone level: iOS Settings → Leti → Notifications → Allow Notifications.

Microphone and sound on logs

When you photograph a log, Leti records a short clip of that moment — up to 10 seconds — so friends can hear it too. You'll see a live indicator the whole time it's recording, and you can play it back and remove it before you post. If you'd rather not use it at all, decline the microphone permission (or turn it off in iOS Settings → Leti → Microphone) and everything else works exactly the same — your logs are just silent.

Privacy and who sees your posts

You choose the audience for every log: your followers, a specific team, everyone, or only you. You can make your whole account private in Settings, so new followers have to be approved first.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Something's broken

There's a feedback button in Settings inside the app — it reaches us within seconds and is the fastest way to report a bug. Tell us what you were doing when it happened and what phone you're on, and we'll get on it.

Copyright

If you believe content on Leti infringes your copyright, email benwodonnell@gmail.com with a description of the work, a link to or screenshot of the content, your contact details, and a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorized. We remove infringing content and terminate repeat infringers.
